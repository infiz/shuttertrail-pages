Simple by design

1. Record your GPS trail. Before you start taking photos, open Shutter Trail and turn on Record Trail. The app records your iPhone’s location as you move. Use High Accuracy when you want more consistent background coverage, then turn it off when the shoot ends to save battery.

2. Automatically geotag your photos. After the shoot, create a Trip for the time range you need and export its GPX file. Use the free Shutter Trail Lightroom plug-in to match selected photos with recorded locations by capture time—even when your travels cross time zones.
